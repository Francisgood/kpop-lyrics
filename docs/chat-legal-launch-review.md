# Fan chat launch: legal and safety issue check

Reviewed 2026-09-28 against the chat PR and public regulator guidance. This is issue spotting for a company decision and legal review, not a certification of compliance. Keep production posting disabled until the launch decisions below are recorded.

## What is already implemented

- Posting needs a shared Aegyo account, acceptance of versioned Fan Chat Terms, and a declaration of age 16 or older. Reading is public. No birth date is collected for chat.
- Posts are screened before publication. Messages can be held for review, signed-in members can report visible messages, and moderators can remove messages and mute accounts for 24 hours.
- The privacy notice describes public messages, automated screening, and 30/90-day deletion. This review updates its effective date and names OpenAI as the screening provider.

## Decisions required before public posting

1. **Audience and territories.** Decide where chat is offered and whether the site is general-audience or likely to attract children under 13. A 16+ checkbox is a participation rule, not proof of age. The FTC says a teen service can still be child-directed depending on its actual audience and presentation, and public chat can disclose personal information. If the company learns that a poster is under 13, it needs an actionable removal/privacy response. A US-first launch still needs this US assessment. [FTC COPPA FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions), [FTC compliance plan](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-six-step-compliance-plan-your-business).
2. **UK/EU scope.** If the service has the relevant UK links, Ofcom requires an assessment of children's access and, where applicable, risk and protection measures. A self-declared age checkbox should not be assumed to establish that children cannot access the room. If EU platform rules apply, counsel should assess the DSA's moderation-terms, illegal-content reporting, decision-reason and redress requirements. The DSA has size-dependent exceptions for some additional platform duties; applicability must be assessed rather than assumed. [Ofcom scope](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/protecting-people-in-the-uk-from-illegal-online-content), [Ofcom children's access](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/childrens-access-assessment-duties-under-the-online-safety-act), [EU DSA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32022R2065).
3. **Legal copy and contact.** Have the company's responsible legal reviewer approve the Fan Chat Terms and the updated Privacy Policy. The current fan rules cover conduct and screening but do not provide a moderation appeal or a staffed safety contact. The existing privacy notice refers to site-wide “Terms of Service,” but no general Terms page was found in this checkout. Decide whether one exists elsewhere and where reports, appeals, privacy requests, and urgent safety concerns should go. Do not promise a response route until the inbox is monitored.
4. **Known underage users.** Name the operator who handles a report that a poster is under 16, especially under 13. The current moderator UI only offers a 24-hour mute. Decide how to prevent further posting and remove or retain affected records under the applicable rules; do not rely on the message text filter alone.
5. **Human coverage.** Assign a primary moderator and backup with access to `/admin/chat`. The current system has no alert to a human when a message is reported or held, and one report does not hide a visible message. Agree on several scheduled checks each day, active coverage during the first event, and an urgent contact route; add alerts or limit posting hours if that coverage cannot be maintained for a 24/7 room. Document escalation for threats/self-harm, sexual content involving minors, personal information, and suspected underage accounts, plus who can disable `AEGYO_CHAT_ENABLED` if screening or coverage fails. AI screening and user reports are support tools, not a substitute for this owner.

### Geographic rollout option (owner preference, not yet enforced)

The owner prefers chat availability throughout the Americas, with Asian countries considered later, while excluding the UK and EU. A country allowlist is clearer for a first release than an open-ended “everywhere except UK/EU” rule; choose the initial country list and review any expansion. Blocking those regions alone does not establish compliance elsewhere, and IP location is imperfect.

The live `www.aegyoarena.com` host currently routes directly to Railway. Railway's edge-location header is not a reliable visitor-country signal. Cloudflare country/path WAF rules would require proxying the host through Cloudflare and confirming the origin cannot be reached around the rule. Any restriction must cover the chat widget, `/chat`, and every chat read/write API on both `aegyoarena.com` and `www.aegyoarena.com`, while keeping the rest of the site available. Test allowed and blocked locations before treating it as a launch control. [Railway edge header](https://docs.railway.com/networking/edge-networking), [Cloudflare proxy status](https://developers.cloudflare.com/dns/proxy-status/), [Cloudflare country rules](https://developers.cloudflare.com/waf/custom-rules/use-cases/allow-traffic-from-specific-countries/).
